StepTato

Walk. Earn. Grow.

A gamified concept app that makes walking the fun choice for short, suitable trips. Your potato levels up as you walk, from couch potato to fit potato. Khurnemekh Altansukh · MSc Urban Data Analytics · University College Dublin

Concept mock-ups of a visual prototype. Names, prices and step counts are illustrative.

The idea

Short, walkable trips around campus still often go by bus or car. TFI Smarter Travel aims to make active travel the easy choice, and these short trips are the easiest win.

Research question: can gamification make people more likely to choose walking for suitable short journeys?

Walk a quest→Earn Sprouts→Grow your Tato→Connect

UCD-connected online survey, 96 responses (52/96, 73/95, 66/95, 45/95). Self-reported intentions, not measured behaviour.

Grow it together

Every walk adds Sprouts to your school's virtual garden, from seedling to garden to grove, and every school feeds one UCD-wide grove. Schools are ranked per active walker, so small schools can win too.

40% said sharing progress or challenges with friends would get them walking more. The garden is virtual; it doesn't plant real trees.

Limits and what's next

This is a visual prototype built on stated intentions, and no app can fix the weather. A small pilot with user testing would show whether people really walk more.

One school→All of UCD→Other campuses

With official backing, Sprouts could unlock real partner rewards.

Small steps. Big change.
